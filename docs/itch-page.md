## Description (paste)
Survive fifteen minutes in Maple Hollow while the dead pour in from every street. Your weapons fire on their own: you
move, you dash, you choose what to grab. Lou Vesper, the last DJ still on the air at K-DED 88.1, calls in jobs that pay
out if you pull them off.

**This free demo has:**
- **Maple Hollow, in three Nights.** Night 1 ends at the 5:00 boss, Night 2 at the 10:00 one, and Night 3 is the full
  night with the final boss.
- **Two survivors:** Rook the ex-cop and Juno the courier.
- **Weapons, gear and evolutions** to find and combine, plus the first rank of every Safehouse upgrade.
- **Things to do between objectives:** houses to loot, street lamps to power, a workbench and a radio relay to bring
  back online.

**The full game** (coming to Steam) adds St. Agnes Hospital and the Quarantine Zone, Hard and Nightmare, four more
survivors, the whole Safehouse and the Daily Outbreak.

**Controls**
- Move: WASD or the arrow keys (touch: drag anywhere)
- Dash: Space
- Ultimate: E
- Hold Out at the evac: H
- Pause: Esc or P

Progress saves in your browser. Once the page has loaded, the game needs no internet connection and tracks nothing.
