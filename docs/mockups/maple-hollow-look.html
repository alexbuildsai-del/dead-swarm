What's new here

  • Dusk falls during the run. Daylight at the start, evening by about 8:00, so early loot is easy to see.
  • Street lamps come on block by block once you power a generator. Zombies in the light move slower.
  • Lootable houses have their porch light on. Stand at the door for 2.5 s; a few zombies burst out.
  • Minimap in the corner, plus a bigger arrow pointing at the next generator.
  • Pickups each get their own shape. Health is a white tin with a big red cross.

On the ground

The proposed pickup designs, drawn the size they'd be on screen ×4. Red now only ever means health.
